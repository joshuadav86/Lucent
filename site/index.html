200 visuals

Every frame is drawn live on your graphics card.

Each visual listens to the bass, mids, treble and beat of what's playing. Physically lit scenes with real reflections, hundreds of thousands of particles per frame, and endless fractals and kaleidoscopes.

Auto mode lands on the downbeat

Lucent studies each sound ahead of time and changes visual at the start of a phrase, with morphs that melt, glow, fly or fold one into the next.

Find one in seconds

Press V and start typing. Visuals sit in family folders with tags like Calm, Intense, Neon and Space. Star your favorites and Auto sticks to them.

Up to 4K, with HDR

Render at 1080p, 1440p or 4K supersampled down, from Low to Ultra detail with up to four times the particles. Highlights shine past white on HDR screens.

Audio editor

Edit, repair and measure without leaving your player.

Right-click any sound and choose Open in editor. Cut it, clean it up, check its loudness and master it with studio meters and your own VST3 effects. Every change has undo, and nothing is saved over the original until you say so.

Cut, copy and paste

Joins crossfade so nothing clicks, Snap lands selections on zero crossings, and paste works between sounds and other apps.

Analyze

Peak, true peak, loudness, loudness range, DC offset, stereo correlation and spectrum, plus a finder that jumps to every clipped spot.

Meters and views

A level meter with true peak and LUFS as you play or loop a section, and a spectrogram view in place of the waveform.

Repair

Take out 50 or 60 Hz hum, clicks and crackle, background noise and DC offset.

EQ and dynamics

A 7-band parametric EQ, a compressor and a true-peak limiter, all heard live, next to your own VST3 effects.

Tempo and pitch

Tempo from 50 to 200%, keeping the pitch or not, or the pitch alone in 0.1 semitone steps.

Channels and markers

Swap, invert, mix to mono, widen the stereo or edit one side alone, and drop markers to jump between parts.

Batch it

The converter can normalize, limit, EQ, compress, de-hum, trim silence and fade every sound in a batch, with tags and artwork carried across.

Analyzer

See the whole sound, not just this second.

A scrolling spectrogram heatmap of everything you play, with a waveform, a live spectrum and a stereo spread scope. Press Y to open it, and point at the heatmap to read any frequency.

Holds still while the music is paused, so you can look closer. Docks with the other windows and follows your skin and theme.

Studio equalizer

Faders like a mixing desk, over the music's live spectrum.

  • 4, 8, 16, 24 or 32 bands. Switching keeps your curve, and the new faders rise into place.
  • See problem frequencies as peaks behind the response curve.
  • 15 presets like Bass Boost, Vocal, Late Night and Small Speakers, or save your own.
  • Safe boosting. Lucent lowers the preamp a touch when you boost, so nothing distorts.
