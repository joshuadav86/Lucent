## What you can do

- Fork, change, and release the code, including in paid or closed-source apps, as the MIT license allows.
- Say your app is "based on Lucent" or "forked from Lucent" to describe where it came from.
- Use the name to talk about Lucent itself, in reviews, articles, tutorials, and links.

## What needs permission

- Releasing a modified version, or any other app, under the name Lucent or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the Lucent logo or app icon in your own app, website, or store listing.
- Suggesting your version is official, or made or endorsed by the Lucent project.

If you release your own version, give it a new name and icon, and make clear it isn't the official Lucent.

To ask for permission, [open an issue](https://github.com/joshuadav86/Lucent/issues).
